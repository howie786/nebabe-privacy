Privacy Policy for ንባብ – Nebabe

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

ንባብ – Nebabe ("Nebabe", "we", "our", or "the app") is an Ethiopian reading and publishing platform designed to connect readers and authors.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nebabe may collect, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Nebabe, we may collect:

  • Name or profile name
  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID
  • Account role such as Reader, Author, or Administrator
  • Profile information
  • Uploaded PDF books
  • Book cover images
  • Book information such as title, author, category, and description
  • Author posts
  • Reading progress
  • Favorite books
  • Highlights
  • Coffee Time reflections
  • Authors you follow
  • Blocked authors
  • Content reports and report reasons
  • Account deletion requests

Some information is optional and is only collected when you choose to use the related feature.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide Reader and Author features
  • Store and display reading progress
  • Store favorites, highlights, and reflections
  • Allow authors to upload and manage books
  • Allow authors to publish posts
  • Display public author profiles
  • Provide follow and block functionality
  • Review books before publication
  • Process reports of inappropriate content
  • Moderate the Nebabe community
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Respond to account and data deletion requests
  • Improve the reliability of the app

4. User-Generated Content

Nebabe allows users, particularly authors, to submit content. This may include books, book covers, author profiles, and posts.

Some submitted content may become publicly visible to other Nebabe users after publication or administrator approval.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the legal right to upload or publish their content.

5. Reading Activity

Nebabe may save information related to your reading experience, including reading progress, favorites, highlights, reflections, and reading activity.

This information is used to provide features such as Continue Reading, reading statistics, Coffee Time, and your personal reading profile.

6. Firebase and Service Providers

Nebabe uses Google Firebase services to operate important parts of the application.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Cloud Functions

These services may process or store information on our behalf to provide authentication, database, storage, security, and application functionality.

7. Data Sharing

Nebabe does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase where necessary to operate the app.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or maintain the security of Nebabe.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information.

Nebabe uses authenticated access and Firebase security mechanisms to help protect account and application data.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Nebabe services, maintain the user's account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the platform.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Nebabe users can request account deletion from inside the app.

The deletion option can be accessed through the account or profile area of Nebabe.

You may also review our account deletion instructions here:

Nebabe Account Deletion

When an account deletion request is completed, account information and associated data will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

Nebabe is not designed specifically for children under 13.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Nebabe features, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
