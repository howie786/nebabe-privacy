Account Deletion

Nebabe users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

You can request account deletion directly from the Nebabe Android app.

Open Nebabe and go to:

Profile → Account & Data → Request Account Deletion

Request Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access the Nebabe app, you may request account deletion by contacting Nebabe Support.

Send an email from the same email address associated with your Nebabe account.

Subject:

Nebabe Account Deletion Request

Contact:

YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL

Request Account Deletion

What Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is completed, Nebabe will delete or remove account-related information where applicable, including:

  • Your Nebabe account profile
  • Your account role and settings
  • Reading progress
  • Favorites
  • Highlights
  • Coffee Time reflections
  • Following and blocking information
  • Associated user-generated data where applicable

Author books or posts may also be removed according to Nebabe's content and account deletion procedures.

Data We May Need to Retain

Certain limited information may be retained when necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Any required retention practices are described in the Nebabe Privacy Policy.
